# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What is expected

- Treat people with respect, including when you think they are wrong.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that maintainers work on this in their spare time, and that "no" is a legitimate answer.
- Remember that most contributors here are writing in a second language. Read for meaning, not for phrasing.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any other space where you are participating in this project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to husni.adil@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately. As the maintainer is also the person who would receive your report, expect a human response rather than a process — and if the problem is with the maintainer, saying so plainly is fair and will be taken seriously.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that do not follow this code, and may block accounts for repeated or severe violations.

## Attribution

This is a plain-language code of conduct in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org). Where this document is silent, the Covenant's intent applies.
